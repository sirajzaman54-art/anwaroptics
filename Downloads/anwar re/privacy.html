Privacy

Draft — owner and legal review pending. This page is a factual, plain-language description of how this website currently handles information. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer and should not be relied on as a finished legal policy until Anwar Optical has had it reviewed and approved.

What this site stores in your browser

  • Your cart (products, quantities, chosen prescription method) is saved in your browser's local storage so it survives a page refresh. It is not sent to any server or seen by anyone until you choose to send it.
  • Recently viewed products and Frame Finder answers are also saved in your browser only, to make browsing more convenient.

What this site does not store

  • Prescription values you enter in the Prescription Decoder or Will It Fit tools are never saved to your browser's storage and are cleared when you leave or refresh the page.
  • This site has no account system and does not store passwords.

What happens when you place an order

The /order page asks only for your name, phone number, city, and any optional notes. This information is used only to generate a WhatsApp message that you review and choose to send to Anwar Optical. It is not stored on a server or shared with anyone else. Your exact delivery address is not collected on this site at all — you share it directly with Anwar Optical in the WhatsApp chat once your order is confirmed.

WhatsApp

Ordering and support conversations happen over WhatsApp, a service operated by Meta. Messages you send there are subject to WhatsApp's own privacy practices, not this website's.

Questions

For any question about your information, contact Anwar Optical directly at WhatsApp or Info@AnwarOptical.com.
