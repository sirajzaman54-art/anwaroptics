Lens care & ordering guidance

Wearing contact lenses safely.

  • Always wash and dry your hands before handling contact lenses.
  • Follow the wear and replacement schedule that comes with your lenses.
  • Stop wearing and remove your lenses if you feel irritation, redness or discomfort.
  • Speak with an eye care professional if you have an existing eye condition or are unsure whether contact lenses are right for you.

This is general guidance only, not a medical recommendation. We cannot advise on suitability for specific eye conditions — please consult an eye care professional.
