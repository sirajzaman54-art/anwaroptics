Understand your prescription

A simple guide to the common numbers and abbreviations you may see on an eyeglass prescription.

This tool explains common prescription terminology. It does not verify, diagnose, or determine whether a prescription is correct.

OD — Right eye

"Oculus Dexter," the standard abbreviation for the right eye on a prescription.

OS — Left eye

"Oculus Sinister," the standard abbreviation for the left eye.

SPH — Sphere

Describes the main lens power listed on a prescription.

CYL — Cylinder

Used when astigmatism correction is included.

AXIS

The orientation of cylinder correction, usually shown as a number from 1–180.

ADD

Additional near power sometimes shown for multifocal prescriptions.

PD — Pupillary distance

The distance between your pupils, measured in millimetres. Need to find yours? Try Measure Your PD.
